When is possession?
Possession begins from December 2026.
What is the price of a 2 BHK?
2 BHK flats start from ₹30.99 lakh (T&C apply). The final price depends on the tower and floor. Call or WhatsApp us, or visit the site, for the exact price of the unit you like.
What are the flat sizes?
There are three 2 BHK layouts: Type I in Towers A, B and C (533 sq.ft RERA carpet), Type II in Towers D and E (553 sq.ft) and Type III in Towers F and G (573 sq.ft). Every flat has two bathrooms, a wash area and a balcony. See the floor plans.
Is the project RERA registered?
Yes. GujRERA registration no. PR/GJ/VADODARA/VADODARA/Others/MAA09582/271221. You can check it on gujrera.gujarat.gov.in.
Are any shops still available?
No. All 21 shops in the retail podium are sold. 2 BHK flats are still available.
Is there parking?
Yes, Seven Sky has basement parking for residents. Ask our team about allotment when you book.
Can you help with a home loan?
Yes. Tell us when you visit, and our team will guide you on home-loan options and the paperwork. You can also try our EMI calculator to plan your budget.
How do I book a site visit?
Site visits are free and available seven days a week. Fill in the form below, call 74338 86565 or 74338 86060, or send "VISIT" on WhatsApp, and we'll confirm a time that suits you.
